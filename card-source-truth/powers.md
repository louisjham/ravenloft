# Powers Source of Truth

This file contains the verbatim text transcribed from the physical Hero Power cards. Any changes or updates should be made here first, and then propagated to the game data files.

## Powers

**HEALING WORD**
*   **Card Type:** CLERIC POWER (UTILITY)
*   **Flavor Text:** You whisper a brief prayer, and a divine healing light washes over your companion.
*   **Rule Text:** Use this power during your Hero Phase. One Hero regains Hit Points equal to his or her healing surge value.

**DIVINE FLARE**
*   **Card Type:** CLERIC POWER (AT-WILL)
*   **Flavor Text:** The light of your deity scorches your foe and empowers your ally.
*   **Rule Text:** Attack one Monster on your tile. If you hit, end one Condition on a Hero on your tile.
*   **Attack:** +6
*   **Damage:** 1

**HEALING STRIKE**
*   **Card Type:** CLERIC POWER (AT-WILL)
*   **Flavor Text:** You smite your foe and heal a nearby companion.
*   **Rule Text:** Attack one adjacent Monster. If you hit, choose one Hero within 1 tile of you. That Hero regains 1 Hit Point.
*   **Attack:** +8
*   **Damage:** 1

**LANCE OF FAITH**
*   **Card Type:** CLERIC POWER (AT-WILL)
*   **Flavor Text:** You sear your foe with a brilliant ray of golden radiance.
*   **Rule Text:** Attack one Monster within 1 tile of you. If the Monster is Undead, you deal +1 damage with this attack.
*   **Attack:** +6
*   **Damage:** 1

**BEACON OF HOPE**
*   **Card Type:** CLERIC POWER (DAILY)
*   **Flavor Text:** You blast your foes with radiant light, and your allies are heartened by the attack.
*   **Rule Text:** Choose a tile within 1 tile of you. Attack each Monster on that tile. Each Hero on the chosen tile regains 1 Hit Point.
*   **Attack:** +6
*   **Damage:** 2

**FLAME STRIKE**
*   **Card Type:** CLERIC POWER (DAILY)
*   **Flavor Text:** A column of divine flame engulfs your foes.
*   **Rule Text:** Choose a tile within 2 tiles of you. Attack each Monster on that tile. Flame Strike does 3 damage, or 1 damage even if the attack misses.
*   **Attack:** +6
*   **Damage:** 3

**HALLOWED ADVANCE**
*   **Card Type:** CLERIC POWER (DAILY)
*   **Flavor Text:** The power of your god inspires your allies to action.
*   **Rule Text:** Attack one adjacent Monster. Hit or miss, each other Hero can move 1 tile. Miss: 1 Damage.
*   **Attack:** +5
*   **Damage:** 3

**BLESS**
*   **Card Type:** CLERIC POWER (UTILITY)
*   **Flavor Text:** You call forth a holy blessing for you and your companions.
*   **Rule Text:** Use this power during your Hero Phase. Each Hero within 1 tile of you gains a +2 bonus to attack rolls until the end of your next Hero Phase.

**CONSECRATED GROUND**
*   **Card Type:** CLERIC POWER (UTILITY)
*   **Flavor Text:** You sanctify the ground around you, warding it from evil.
*   **Rule Text:** Use this power during your Hero Phase instead of attacking. Choose a tile within 1 tile of you. If there is a marker on that tile, discard it. Place the Consecrated Ground marker on the chosen tile. The next time you would place an Encounter marker on that tile, discard that Encounter Card and do not draw a new Encounter Card. Then discard the Consecrated Ground marker.

**SHIELD OF FAITH**
*   **Card Type:** CLERIC POWER (UTILITY)
*   **Flavor Text:** Divine energy protects you and your companions.
*   **Rule Text:** Use this power when you or a Hero on your tile is hit by an attack. The attack misses instead. You and each Hero within 1 tile of you gains a +2 bonus to AC until the end of your next Hero Phase.

**DRAGON'S BREATH**

*   **Flavor Text:** With a roar, you breathe a deadly blast of fire that engulfs your foes.
*   **Rule Text:** Attack each Monster on your tile. This attack does not count as an attack action.

*   **Damage:** 1

**CLEAVE**

*   **Rule Text:** Attack one adjacent Monster. If you hit, choose another Monster on your tile and move adjacent to it. That Monster takes 1 damage.

The Illusionary Crowd counts as a Hero when a Monster chooses a target. The Illusionary Crowd has AC 14 and 1 HP. Remove the Illusionary Crowd marker when it is damaged.
