# Monsters Source of Truth

This file contains the verbatim text transcribed from the physical Monster cards. Any changes or updates should be made here first, and then propagated to the game data files.

## Monsters

**BLAZING SKELETON**
*   **Card Type:** MONSTER (UNDEAD)
*   **Trigger:** TACTICS (AC: 13, HP: 2)
*   **Rule Text:** If the Blazing Skeleton is within 1 tile of a Hero, it attacks each Hero on the closest Hero's tile with a ball of fire. Otherwise, the Blazing Skeleton moves 1 tile toward the closest Hero. (Attack: +7, Damage: 2, Miss: 1 Damage)
*   **Resolve Text:** 2 EXPERIENCE

**GARGOYLE**
*   **Card Type:** MONSTER (ELEMENTAL)
*   **Trigger:** TACTICS (AC: 16, HP: 2)
*   **Rule Text:** If the Gargoyle is within 1 tile of a Hero, it moves to the closest Hero's tile and attacks each Hero on the tile with a whirlwind of claws. Otherwise, the Gargoyle does nothing. (Attack: +8, Damage: 2 and Slowed, Miss: 1 Damage)
*   **Resolve Text:** 3 EXPERIENCE

**GHOUL**

(Attack: +7, Damage: 1 and Immobilized) Otherwise, the Ghoul moves 1 tile toward the closest Hero.

**DARK ACOLYTE**
*   **Card Type:** MONSTER (HUMAN)
*   **Trigger:** TACTICS (AC: 14, HP: 3)
*   **Rule Text:** If a hero is on the same tile, Dark Acolyte teleports to the furthest tile with an unexplored edge. Pass this card to the 2nd player on your left. Otherwise, attack the closest hero within 2 tiles with necrotic blast. Pass this card to the 2nd player on your left. (Attack: +7, Damage: 1 and hero is Slowed)
*   **Special Ability:** **Dark chant:** if Dark Acolyte did not attack, the closest wounded Undead regains 1 HP.
*   **Resolve Text:** 4 EXPERIENCE

**DREAD WARRIOR**
*   **Card Type:** MONSTER (UNDEAD)
*   **Trigger:** TACTICS (AC: 16, HP: 2)
*   **Rule Text:** If Dread Warrior is within 1 tile of a hero, move adjacent to the most number of heroes possible and attack a hero with it's axe. Otherwise, move 1 tile towards the closest hero. (Attack: +6, Damage: 2)
*   **Special Ability:** **Tireless pursuit:** if a hero started his turn adjacent to Dread Warrior and moves away, move adjacent to the hero and attack with it's axe.
*   **Resolve Text:** 2 EXPERIENCE

**GRAVEHOUND**
*   **Card Type:** MONSTER (UNDEAD)
*   **Trigger:** TACTICS (AC: 14, HP: 1)
*   **Rule Text:** If Grave Hound is within 2 tiles of the hero with the lowest total HP, move adjacent to the hero and attack with bite. Otherwise, move 2 tiles towards the hero with the lowest total HP. (Attack: +9, Damage: 1)
*   **Resolve Text:** 1 EXPERIENCE

**MUMMY**
*   **Card Type:** MONSTER (UNDEAD)
*   **Trigger:** TACTICS (AC: 14, HP: 4)
*   **Rule Text:** If Mummy is within 1 tile of a hero, move adjacent to the closest hero and attack with rotting touch. Pass this card to 2nd player on your left. Otherwise, move 1 tile towards the closest hero. Pass this card to the 2nd player on your left. (Attack: +6, Damage: 1)
*   **Special Ability:** **Mummy rot:** Damage inflicted by Mummy cannot be healed (turn the wound token face down).
*   **Resolve Text:** 4 EXPERIENCE

**SKULL LORD**
*   **Card Type:** MONSTER (UNDEAD)
*   **Trigger:** TACTICS (AC: 15, HP: 3)
*   **Rule Text:** If Skull Lord is within 1 tile of a hero, attack a hero within 1 tile for each remaining HP on Skull Lord. Pass this card to the 2nd player on your left. Otherwise, move 1 tile towards the nearest hero. Pass this card to the 2nd player on your left. (Attack: +7, Damage: 1 and Hero is Slowed)
*   **Special Ability:** **Cackling skulls:** Any hero that attacks and misses Skull Lord from within 1 tile must flip a daily/utility power or take 1 damage.
*   **Resolve Text:** 4 EXPERIENCE

**VAMPIRE BAT**
*   **Card Type:** MONSTER (ANIMAL)
*   **Trigger:** TACTICS (AC: 16, HP: 1)
*   **Rule Text:** If adjacent to a hero, attack with bite. (Attack: +5, Damage: 1) If on a tile with an unexplored edge and no heroes, reveal a tile from the bottom of the stack on all unexplored edges. Do not draw Encounter cards. Otherwise, move 1 tile towards the closest hero.
*   **Resolve Text:** 1 EXPERIENCE
