# Environments Source of Truth

This file contains the verbatim text transcribed from the physical Environment cards. Any changes or updates should be made here first, and then propagated to the game data files.

## Environments

**BAT SWARM**
*   **Card Type:** ENVIRONMENT
*   **Flavor Text:** A horde of shrieking bats flutter through the catacombs, blocking your sight.
*   **Environment Rule:** If there is an Environment Card already in play, discard it. This Environment remains in play until a new Environment Card is drawn.
*   **Card Rule:** Whenever a Hero attacks a Monster that is not on his or her tile, that Hero makes the attack roll twice and uses the lower result.

**BLOOD FOG**
*   **Card Type:** ENVIRONMENT
*   **Flavor Text:** A mystical reddish fog brings out the inner animal in the living and the dead.
*   **Environment Rule:** If there is an Environment Card already in play, discard it. This Environment remains in play until a new Environment Card is drawn.
*   **Card Rule:** Whenever a Hero or a Monster attacks and rolls a natural 17 or higher, the target of the attack takes 1 additional damage.

**CACKLING SKULL**
*   **Card Type:** ENVIRONMENT
*   **Flavor Text:** A crystal skull floats out of the darkness. Its maddening laughter makes it difficult to focus.
*   **Environment Rule:** If there is an Environment Card already in play, discard it. This Environment remains in play until a new Environment Card is drawn.
*   **Card Rule:** Whenever a Hero uses a Daily power or Utility power, that Hero takes 1 damage.

**CRIPPLING MIASMA**
*   **Card Type:** ENVIRONMENT
*   **Flavor Text:** A horrible stench of decay fills the air.
*   **Environment Rule:** If there is an Environment Card already in play, discard it. This Environment remains in play until a new Environment Card is drawn.
*   **Card Rule:** Each Hero's speed is reduced by 1. (Slowed Heroes are unaffected.)

**DEADLY SHADOWS**
*   **Card Type:** ENVIRONMENT
*   **Flavor Text:** Voices from the shadows whisper horrifying secrets about your companions.
*   **Environment Rule:** If there is an Environment Card already in play, discard it. This Environment remains in play until a new Environment Card is drawn.
*   **Card Rule:** Whenever the active Hero ends his or her turn on a tile that has another Hero on it, the active Hero takes 1 damage.

**HAUNTED MISTS**
*   **Card Type:** ENVIRONMENT
*   **Flavor Text:** You catch glimpses of the faces of long dead adventurers in the mists that fill the crypt.
*   **Environment Rule:** If there is an Environment Card already in play, discard it. This Environment remains in play until a new Environment Card is drawn.
*   **Card Rule:** Undead Monsters gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls.

**MUSIC OF THE DAMNED**
*   **Card Type:** ENVIRONMENT
*   **Flavor Text:** The haunting sound of an organ echoes through the crypts, sending shivers down your spine.
*   **Environment Rule:** If there is an Environment Card already in play, discard it. This Environment remains in play until a new Environment Card is drawn.
*   **Card Rule:** Whenever a Hero would place a new Monster, that Hero draws two Monster Cards and chooses the Monster worth the most experience. He or she places that Monster and discards the other.

**SPIRIT OF DOOM**
*   **Card Type:** ENVIRONMENT
*   **Flavor Text:** A grim specter materializes nearby, bringing portents of impending doom.
*   **Environment Rule:** If there is an Environment Card already in play, discard it. This Environment remains in play until a new Environment Card is drawn.
*   **Card Rule:** Whenever a Hero discards a Treasure Card, he or she takes 1 damage.
