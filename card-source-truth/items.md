# Items Source of Truth

This file contains the verbatim text transcribed from the physical Item cards. Any changes or updates should be made here first, and then propagated to the game data files.

## Items

**SILVER DAGGER**
*   **Card Type:** ITEM
*   **Trigger:** Use this item during your Hero Phase instead of attacking.
*   **Rule Text:** Attack one Werewolf within 2 tiles of you. Hit or miss, the Werewolf no longer regains Hit Points from its Regeneration ability for the rest of the game. (Attack: +5, Damage: 3, Miss: 1 Damage)
*   **Resolve Text:** Discard this card after using it.

**THIEVES' TOOLS**
*   **Card Type:** ITEM
*   **Trigger:** Play this item immediately.
*   **Rule Text:** You gain a +4 bonus to rolls to disable Traps while this item is in play.
*   **Resolve Text:** None

**SUNSWORD**
*   **Card Type:** ITEM
*   **Trigger:** Play this item immediately.
*   **Rule Text:** You deal +1 damage against adjacent Vampires while this item is in play.
*   **Resolve Text:** None

**TOME OF STRAHD**
*   **Card Type:** ITEM
*   **Trigger:** Play this item immediately.
*   **Rule Text:** You and each Hero within 1 tile of you gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls against Vampires while this item is in play.
*   **Resolve Text:** None

**TORCH** *(Note: 2 Copies Transcribed)*
*   **Card Type:** ITEM
*   **Trigger:** Use this item during your Hero Phase instead of attacking.
*   **Rule Text:** Attack each Monster on your tile. Hit or miss, place each Monster on your tile on a tile within 1 tile of you. (Attack: +5, Damage: 1)
*   **Resolve Text:** Discard this card after using it.

**WAND OF TELEPORTATION**
*   **Card Type:** ITEM
*   **Trigger:** Use this item during your Hero Phase instead of attacking.
*   **Rule Text:** Choose a tile within 1 tile of you. Place each Monster on the chosen tile on a tile within 3 tiles of you.
*   **Resolve Text:** Discard this card after using it.

**WOODEN STAKE**
*   **Card Type:** ITEM
*   **Trigger:** Use this item during your Hero Phase instead of attacking.
*   **Rule Text:** Attack one adjacent Vampire. **Hit or miss**, the Vampire does not activate during your next Villain Phase. (Attack: +5, Damage: 3, Miss: 1 Damage)
*   **Resolve Text:** Discard this card after using it.
