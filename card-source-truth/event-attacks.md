# Event-Attacks Source of Truth

This file contains the verbatim text transcribed from the physical Event-Attack cards. Any changes or updates should be made here first, and then propagated to the game data files.

## Event-Attacks

**AMBUSH!**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT-ATTACK
*   **Flavor Text:** A massive net falls from the ceiling while monsters close in from all sides.
*   **Card Rules:** Attack each Hero on the active Hero's tile. Place a new Monster on the active Hero's tile.
*   **ATK Bonus:** +8
*   **Damage:** Slowed
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**ANIMATED ARMOR**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT-ATTACK
*   **Flavor Text:** Suits of armor spring out of dark alcoves to attack you.
*   **Card Rules:** Attack each Hero on the active Hero's tile.
*   **ATK Bonus:** +7
*   **Damage:** 3 (Miss: 1 Damage)
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**CIRCLE OF DEATH**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT-ATTACK
*   **Flavor Text:** A wave of shadowy, pulse-stopping energy blossoms from the center of the room.
*   **Card Rules:** Attack each Hero on the active Hero's tile. Each Undead Monster on the active Hero's tile regains 1 Hit Point.
*   **ATK Bonus:** +8
*   **Damage:** 2 (Miss: 1 Damage)
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**GRAY OOZE**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT-ATTACK
*   **Flavor Text:** A pseudopod emerges from a puddle of gray ooze on the ground and strikes at you.
*   **Card Rules:** Attack the active Hero. If the active Hero is hit by the attack, the active Hero discards 1 Treasure Card at random.
*   **ATK Bonus:** +8
*   **Damage:** 3 (Miss: 1 Damage)
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**GREEN SLIME**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT-ATTACK
*   **Flavor Text:** A hideous blob of green slime falls from the ceiling
*   **Card Rules:** Attack each Hero on the active Hero's tile. If the active Hero is hit by the attack, the active Hero discards 1 Treasure Card at random.
*   **ATK Bonus:** +6
*   **Damage:** 2 (Miss: 1 Damage)
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**HANDS OF THE DEAD**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT-ATTACK
*   **Flavor Text:** Severed hands crawl from the earth and grab at you.
*   **Card Rules:** Attack each Hero on the active Hero's tile. If a Hero is hit by the attack, the Hero is slowed.
*   **ATK Bonus:** +6
*   **Damage:** 2 (Miss: 1 Damage)
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**HOWLING GHOST**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT-ATTACK
*   **Flavor Text:** A shrieking banshee emerges from the shadows; its howl causes you to recoil in agony.
*   **Card Rules:** Attack each Hero on the active Hero's tile. A Hero hit by this attack can flip an unused Daily power over to prevent this damage.
*   **ATK Bonus:** +9
*   **Damage:** 2
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**ICY CORRIDOR**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT-ATTACK
*   **Flavor Text:** A thin layer of ice makes it difficult to stand in one place.
*   **Card Rules:** Attack each Hero on the active Hero's tile. After the attack, place each Hero on the active Hero's tile on a tile within 1 tile of the active Hero's tile. (Heroes can be placed on different tiles.)
*   **ATK Bonus:** +8
*   **Damage:** 2 (Miss: 1 Damage)
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**KING TOMESCU'S PORTAL**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT-ATTACK
*   **Flavor Text:** You fall through a mysterious portal, reappearing elsewhere in the crypts covered with wounds.
*   **Card Rules:** Attack the active Hero three times. Remove the active Hero from his or her tile. At the start of that Hero's next Hero Phase, place that Hero on any tile.
*   **ATK Bonus:** +8
*   **Damage:** 1
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**PATRINA VELIKOVNA**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT-ATTACK
*   **Flavor Text:** The ghostly image of an elf maid appears before you. She smiles for a moment before unleashing a blood curdling shriek.
*   **Card Rules:** Attack each Hero. After the attack, place each Monster that is not on the same tile as a Hero 1 tile closer to the closest Hero.
*   **ATK Bonus:** +7
*   **Damage:** 2 (Miss: 1 Damage)
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**PROWLING GHOST**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT-ATTACK
*   **Flavor Text:** The ghosts of past adventurers haunt this room. Their shrieks send you running deeper into the dungeon.
*   **Card Rules:** Attack the active Hero. After the attack, draw a tile from the bottom of the Dungeon Tile stack and place it adjacent to the unexplored edge that is closest to the active Hero. Place a new Monster on that tile, and then place the active Hero on that tile.
*   **ATK Bonus:** +9
*   **Damage:** 1
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**SPIDER WEBS**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT-ATTACK
*   **Flavor Text:** Hundreds of tiny spiders crawl across the ceiling, sending a thick mass of webs down upon you.
*   **Card Rules:** Attack the active Hero.
*   **ATK Bonus:** +4
*   **Damage:** Immobilized (Miss: Slowed)
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**STRAHD ATTACKS!**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT-ATTACK
*   **Flavor Text:** Strahd steps from the shadows, sending a ball of fire streaking from his outstretched hands. As it explodes, he disappears back into the shadows.
*   **Card Rules:** Attack each Hero within 1 tile of the active Hero.
*   **ATK Bonus:** +6
*   **Damage:** 3 (Miss: 1 Damage)
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.
