# Events Source of Truth

This file contains the verbatim text transcribed from the physical Event cards. Any changes or updates should be made here first, and then propagated to the game data files.

## Events

**BUBBLING CAULDRON**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** The monsters around you rush forward, drawn by a repulsive odor spilling out of a bubbling cauldron.
*   **Card Rule:** Place each Monster 1 tile closer to the active Hero.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**CORNER OF YOUR EYE**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** You investigate something moving in the darkness.
*   **Card Rule:** Place the active Hero on an adjacent tile and roll the die: 
    *   **1-15:** A Monster rushes you from the darkness. Place a new Monster on the active Hero's tile. 
    *   **16-20:** A friendly spirit inspires you to fight on. Flip up one of your used powers.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**COWARDLY FLIGHT**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** A monster flees from you to seek help.
*   **Card Rule:** If there are no Monsters in play, discard this card. Draw a tile from the bottom of the Dungeon Tile stack and place it adjacent to the unexplored edge that is closest to the active Hero. Place the Monster closest to the active Hero on that tile, and place a new Monster on that tile. Do not draw an Encounter Card.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**CYRUS BELVIEW**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** A gaunt, ghoulish looking man in a torn suit grins at you through yellowed teeth. "The master said we were expecting company. Allow me to escort you to your chambers."
*   **Card Rule:** Draw a tile from the bottom of the Dungeon Tile stack and place it adjacent to any unexplored edge. Place two new Monsters on that tile, then place the active Hero on that tile. Each other Hero can place himself or herself on the active Hero's new tile.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**FRENZY**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** The monsters around you roar in fury.
*   **Card Rule:** Each Monster you control activates twice during your Villain Phase.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**GHOST OF PRINCE AUREL**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** A ghost materializes in the corridor ahead; its horrid features freeze you in terror.
*   **Card Rule:** Flip over one unused Daily power or Utility power. If you don't have an unused power to flip over, take 1 damage instead.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**HOWL OF THE WOLF**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** The distant baying of a wolf echoes through the dungeon, causing the monsters around you to shriek in response.
*   **Card Rule:** Choose one Monster on any Hero's tile. That Monster immediately activates.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**ILLUSIONARY TRICK**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** Even allies aren't always what they appear to be!
*   **Card Rule:** The active Hero swaps positions with the Monster that is farthest from him or her.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**LIEF LIPSIEGE**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** A gnarled old man dressed as a clerk steps from the shadows. "I watch the master's treasure. At least, I do unless I'm distracted."
*   **Card Rule:** You try to distract Lief. Roll a die:
    *   **1-10:** You scare Lief and he calls for help. Place a new Monster on the active Hero's tile.
    *   **11-20:** Draw a Treasure Card.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**MISTS OF TERROR**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** You hear a whispering chant, and a white mist flows through the crypts. As you breathe in the foul air, horrifying thoughts root you in place.
*   **Card Rule:** Roll a die for each Hero. On a 1-5, the Hero takes 1 damage and is Immobilized.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**NEGLECTED PASSAGE**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** "Did you look down that hall?"
*   **Card Rule:** If a Start tile is not in play, discard this card and draw a new Encounter Card. Draw a tile from the bottom of the Dungeon Tile stack and place it adjacent to the unexplored edge that is closest to the Start tile. Place a new Monster on that tile, but do not draw an Encounter Card.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**OVERRUN**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** Monsters charge from all directions!
*   **Card Rule:** Each Hero takes damage equal to the number of Monsters he or she controls.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**OVERWHELMING TERROR**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** A cacophony of shrieks and howls rises up around you, and you flee in terror.
*   **Card Rule:** If a Start tile is not in play, discard this card and draw a new Encounter Card. Place each Hero 2 tiles closer to the Start tile. If a Hero is on the same tile as a Monster after being placed, that Hero is Slowed.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**PASSAGE OF TIME**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** The unholy air in Strahd's crypts saps the strength from you and your companions.
*   **Card Rule:** Each Hero takes 1 damage.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**PROWLING SPIRITS**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** Cold, spectral hands grasp at you. Before you can react, one of your items is gone.
*   **Card Rule:** You must discard one of your Treasure Cards (of your choice).
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**REINFORCEMENTS**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** The constant advance of Strahd's minions gives you no time to relax.
*   **Card Rule:** Choose the Hero controlling the fewest Monsters. That Hero places a new Monster on any tile with an unexplored edge.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**SECRET DOOR**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** A section of the stone wall slides away, revealing a hidden chamber.
*   **Card Rule:** Draw a tile from the bottom of the Dungeon Tile stack and place it adjacent to the unexplored edge that is closest to the active Hero. Place a new Monster on that tile, but do not draw an Encounter Card.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**SPIRIT OF DOOM**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** The entire crypt shudders as though some gargantuan creature was moving deep below.
*   **Card Rule:** Each Hero can immediately move up to his or her Speed. After this move, each Hero on a tile with no Monsters takes 1 damage.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**STRAHD'S HUNGER**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** Vertigo washes over you as Strahd steps from the shadows. His will overwhelms you, and he laughs as he steps back into the darkness.
*   **Card Rule:** Choose the Hero with the most Hit Points remaining. That Hero takes 1 damage and is Immobilized.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**STRAHD'S MINIONS**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** Strahd steps from the shadows and casts a spell.
*   **Card Rule:** Place the active Hero and the two Monsters that are closest to that Hero on the tile farthest from the active Hero. If there are less than two Monsters in play, place a new Monster adjacent to the active Hero after he or she is placed.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**STRAHD'S TRICK**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** Your mind reels as Strahd places a spell upon you. Your friends become enemies, and your enemies become friends.
*   **Card Rule:** Place the active Hero on a tile within 1 tile of you that has the most Monsters on it. If no tile within 1 tile of you has Monsters, the active Hero takes 1 damage.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**STRAHD'S WHISPERS**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** Strahd's voice echoes in your mind, compelling you to turn against your friends.
*   **Card Rule:** Place the active Hero adjacent to the next closest Hero. The active Hero attacks that Hero with an at-will power.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**SUMMONING CIRCLE**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** A careless misstep disturbs a magic rune, freeing a horrid creature.
*   **Card Rule:** Place a new Monster adjacent to the active Hero.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**TELEPORT GLYPH**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** An arcane symbol flashes at your feat and you disappear from sight.
*   **Card Rule:** Place the active Hero and each Monster on his or her tile on the tile farthest from the active Hero.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**TREASURE CHEST**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** You find an ancient chest with a rusty lock.
*   **Card Rule:** You open a treasure chest. Roll a die:
    *   **1-10:** Take 2 damage.
    *   **11-15:** Take 1 damage and draw 1 Treasure Card.
    *   **16-20:** Draw 1 Treasure Card.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.

**VOICE OF THE MASTER**
*   **Card Type:** EVENT
*   **Flavor Text:** Strahd's voice echoes through the catacombs. "Slay them, my minions!" The monsters around you surge forward to attack.
*   **Card Rule:** Starting with the Hero on your left, each Hero immediately activates one Monster he or she controls. The first Hero that cannot activate a Monster instead places a new Monster on any tile.
*   **Resolve Rule:** Discard this card.
