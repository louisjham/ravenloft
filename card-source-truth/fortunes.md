# Fortunes Source of Truth

This file contains the verbatim text transcribed from the physical Fortune cards. Any changes or updates should be made here first, and then propagated to the game data files.

---

## Action Surge
> Play this fortune immediately — You can move your speed or make an attack — Discard this card after playing it

---

## Breath Of Life
> Play this fortune immediately — Your Hero regains one hit point — Discard this card after playing it

---

## Burst Of Speed
> Play this fortune immediately — Your Hero can move his or her speed — Discard this card after playing it

---

## Clear The Air
> Play this fortune immediately — Discard an Environment card in play — Discard this card after playing it

---

## Moment's Respite
> Play this fortune immediately — Place this card face-up on top of either the Encounter Deck or the Monster Deck. The next time a card would be drawn from the chosen deck, discard this card instead.

---

## Daze
> Play this fortune immediately — Put this card face-up on a monster card. The next time that monster would activate, discard this card instead.

---

## Distant Sounds
> Play this fortune immediately — Look at the top 3 cards of the monster deck and put them back in any order. — Discard this card after playing it

---

## Eagle Eyes
> Play this fortune immediately — Place one tile from the top of the Dungeon Tile stack adjacent to any unexplored edge. Place any new monsters on that tile as normal but do not draw an encounter card. — Discard this card after playing it

---

## Glimpse Of The Future
> Play this fortune immediately — Look at the top 3 cards of the Encounter Deck and put them back on the top of the deck in any order. — Discard this card after playing it

---

## Harrowed Experience
> Play this fortune immediately — This card counts as 1 experience point. Put in the XP pile, and discard this card when the party uses it for experience. 1 EXPERIENCE

---

## Intimidating Bellow
> Play this fortune immediately — Place one monster on a tile 2 tiles away from its tile — Discard this card after playing it

---

## Level Up
> Play this fortune immediately — You can spend 5 experience points to have your Hero become 2nd level — Discard this card after playing it

---

## Lucky Find
> Play this fortune immediately — Draw three treasure cards and keep one. Discard the others. — Discard this card after playing it

---

## Shake It Off
> Play this fortune immediately — Choose one Hero. That Hero can end one condition affecting him or her. — Discard this card after playing it

---

## Short Rest
> Play this fortune immediately — Flip up one of your used powers. — Discard this card after playing it
